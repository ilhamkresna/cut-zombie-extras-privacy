# CUT! Zombie Extras — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026
**Developer:** Kresna Labs
**Contact:** kresnalabs@gmail.com

This policy explains how Kresna Labs handles information when you play CUT! Zombie Extras (package `com.megajam.cutzombieextras`) on Android.

## Summary

- The game has no account or login. It does not ask for your name, email address or precise location.
- We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes.
- The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob.
- In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.
- We do not sell your data.

## Information we and our service providers process

**Game progress (on your device).** Your progress, coins and reels, unlocked costumes and settings are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this save data.

**Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics).** Firebase Analytics collects app interactions (for example, a run starting or ending and its score), device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version) and an app instance ID. We use this to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

**Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics).** If the game crashes, Crashlytics collects crash logs, diagnostic information and device information. We use this to keep the game stable.

**Ads (Google AdMob).** You can optionally watch a rewarded ad to earn a free retake. AdMob may collect device identifiers, including the advertising ID, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), ads are personalised only subject to your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform. See [Google's partner sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) to learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

**Purchases (Google Play Billing).** If you buy something in the game, Google processes the payment. We receive purchase tokens and order information needed to deliver and verify your purchase. We do not receive your card or payment details.

## How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob and Google Play), which process it under their own policies, including [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit.

## Children

CUT! Zombie Extras is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your choices and data deletion

- **Delete local data:** uninstalling the game removes the save data stored on your device.
- **Request deletion:** email kresnalabs@gmail.com to request deletion of analytics and crash data associated with your device, and include your device model and app version to help us respond.
- **Ad controls:** you can manage ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your Android device's Settings (Google > Ads) and manage Google account data at myaccount.google.com.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated when it changes.
